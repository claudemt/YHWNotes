\begin{derivation}[相关噪声的短时与长时极限]
展开 $e^{-t/\tau_c}\approx 1-t/\tau_c+t^2/(2\tau_c^2)$：
\begin{equation}
t\tau_c-\tau_c^2(1-e^{-t/\tau_c})\approx t\tau_c-\tau_c^2(t/\tau_c-t^2/(2\tau_c^2))=t^2/2.
\end{equation}
故 $D_{\ell\ell'}\simeq e^{-(\ell-\ell')^2\sigma^2t^2/2}$。衰减是高斯型（$t^2$），不是指数型。

$e^{-t/\tau_c}\to 0$：
\begin{equation}
t\tau_c-\tau_c^2\approx t\tau_c.
\end{equation}
故 $D_{\ell\ell'}\simeq e^{-(\ell-\ell')^2\sigma^2\tau_c t}$。衰减是指数型，速率 $\Gamma_{\ell\ell'}=(\ell-\ell')^2\sigma^2\tau_c$。

\end{derivation}
